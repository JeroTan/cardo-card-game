# Game Setup & Zones

## Deck Building Rules

Every deck must follow this **mandatory format** of exactly **50 cards**:

- **Exactly 5 cards** of each ATK/DEF value (0-9)
- No more, no less—this is the core deck building rule
- Example: 5× (0/0), 5× (1/9), 5× (2/8)... 5× (9/1)

## Game Zones Explained

### Personal Zones (Each Player Has)

**1. Hand**
- Your cards available to play
- **Maximum 9 cards** at any time
- Start with 5 cards

**2. Deck**
- Your draw pile (face down)
- Draw cards from here during your turn

**3. Jail (Discard Pile)**
- Where your used cards go
- Defeated Sentinel cards end up here

### Shared Zone (All Players Share)

**Sentinel Zone**
- Contains **1-3 cards** (fused together)
- Has one owner at a time
- The main target of challenges!

![Game Zones](https://placehold.co/600x400/f59e0b/ffffff?text=Game+Zones+Layout)

## Starting the Game

1. Shuffle your 50-card deck
2. Draw 5 cards to form your starting hand
3. The first player declares their Sentinel (1 card from hand)
4. Turns proceed clockwise!

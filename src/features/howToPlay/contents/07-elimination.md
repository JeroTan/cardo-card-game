# Elimination & Winning

## How Players Are Eliminated

![Elimination](https://placehold.co/600x400/dc2626/ffffff?text=Elimination+Rules)

## Elimination Conditions

### 1. Voluntary Surrender
- You choose to surrender during your turn
- Discard your hand to Jail
- You're out of the game

### 2. Forced Elimination
The most common way to lose:

**You are eliminated when:**
- It's your turn
- You **DON'T** own the Sentinel
- You **CANNOT** draw (deck is empty)
- You **CANNOT** make a valid challenge (no cards strong enough)

**Result:** No legal moves = Automatic elimination

### 3. Hand Management Failure
- If you're forced to discard below 0 cards
- Rare, but possible with certain game effects

## Staying Alive

### Key Strategies

**1. Manage Your Deck**
- Don't draw too aggressively early game
- Save cards for critical moments
- Empty deck = risk of elimination!

**2. Calculate Before Attacking**
- Always verify you can win the challenge
- Failed challenge attempts waste your turn
- May leave you vulnerable

**3. Balance Hand Size**
- Maximum 9 cards in hand
- Can't draw if hand is full
- Don't hoard cards unnecessarily

**4. Know When to Strike**
- Wait for weak Sentinels
- Combo cards for powerful attacks
- Timing is everything!

## Winning the Game

The **last player standing** wins! 

**Victory Conditions:**
- All other players eliminated
- You still have legal moves available
- You've outlasted everyone through strategy!

![Victory](https://placehold.co/600x400/22c55e/ffffff?text=Victory!)

Keep your deck alive, pick your battles wisely, and claim victory!

## Deck Building Rules

### Mandatory Format
All decks must follow this format:
- **Exactly 5 cards** of each value (0-9)
- Total: **50 cards** (5 × 10 values)
- This ensures balanced gameplay and fair access to all card types

### What This Means for Strategy
- You'll always have access to the full range of ATK/DEF options
- **5 Zero cards** in every deck—use them wisely!
- **5 high-attack cards (9/1)** for aggressive plays
- **5 high-defense cards (1/9)** for strong Sentinels
- Equal card distribution means victory comes from smart play decisions

## In-Game Tactics

### When Owning the Sentinel

**High DEF Sentinel (1-3 cards)**
- Skip turn and force others to challenge
- They'll need strong combos to defeat you
- Forces opponents to waste cards

**Low DEF Sentinel (7-9 cards)**
- More vulnerable to attacks
- Consider aggressive draws to rebuild
- Prepare for imminent loss

### When Challenging

**Count Their Defense**
- Always calculate Sentinel_DEF before attacking
- Don't waste strong cards on failed attempts
- Combo calculation: `Card1_ATK + Card2_ATK + Card3_ATK`

**Save Your Zeros**
- Zero cards are "get out of jail free" cards
- Use when opponent has ultra-high defense
- Keep as backup when deck runs low

**Multi-Card Fusion Strategy**
- Attacking with 2-3 cards creates stronger Sentinel
- Fused Sentinels have high DEF (sum of all cards)
- Risk: Uses more cards from hand

### Resource Management

**Drawing Strategy**
- Early game: Draw to build hand options
- Mid game: Balance drawing vs challenging
- Late game: Conserve deck—empty deck = death!

**Hand Size Awareness**
- Max 9 cards—can't draw when full
- Discard through challenges strategically
- Don't hoard—use your cards!

## Advanced Tactics

### The "Weak Sentinel Bait"
- Declare low DEF Sentinel (7-9 cards)
- Opponent attacks and takes ownership
- Now **they** defend while you rebuild

### The "Zero Counter"
- If you expect Zero attacks, declare Zero as Sentinel
- Only defense against Zero power
- Trade-off: Vulnerable to normal attacks (0 DEF!)

### Deck Counting
- Track how many cards you've drawn
- Know when you're close to empty deck
- Plan final challenges before elimination risk

### Reading Opponents
- Watch who's drawing vs challenging
- Empty-hand players are desperate
- Players with full hands (9 cards) can't draw

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ **Drawing when hand is full (9 cards)**
❌ **Challenging without calculating first**
❌ **Using Zero card too early**
❌ **Declaring weak Sentinel with low-DEF cards**
❌ **Forgetting you can't challenge after drawing**

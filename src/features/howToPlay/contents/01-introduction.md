# Welcome to CARDO

CARDO is an exciting card game where strategy meets simplicity. Your goal is to be the **last player standing** by challenging and capturing the Sentinel—a powerful shared card zone that changes hands throughout the game.

![Game Overview](https://placehold.co/600x400/3b82f6/ffffff?text=CARDO+Game+Board)

## Game Overview

- **Players:** 2-10 players
- **Deck Size:** Exactly 50 cards
- **Starting Hand:** 5 cards
- **Game Type:** Elimination-based, shared-sentinel mechanics

## What Makes CARDO Unique?

Unlike traditional card games, CARDO features a **Sentinel Zone**—a shared area containing 1-3 cards. Players take turns either defending their Sentinel or challenging others to claim it. The tension builds as each challenge could shift the balance of power!

## Your Objective

To win, you must force your opponents to **empty their decks** and become **incapable of making any action**. Victory comes when you are the **only player left** who can still play—meaning all opponents have been eliminated because they have no cards to draw and no valid moves to make.

Outlast your opponents by strategically managing your hand, calculating attacks, and knowing when to strike!

# Turn Structure

Your available actions depend on whether you **own the Sentinel** or not.

![Turn Actions](https://placehold.co/600x400/ef4444/ffffff?text=Turn+Actions)

## If You Own the Sentinel

As the Sentinel owner, you have a unique position:

### Option 1: Skip Turn
- Simply pass your turn to the next player
- Maintain control of your Sentinel
- Wait for others to challenge you

### Option 2: Surrender
- Give up the game voluntarily
- Your Sentinel goes to Jail
- Next player declares a new Sentinel
- You are eliminated

**Note:** You **cannot** challenge while owning the Sentinel!

---

## If You DON'T Own the Sentinel

You have more aggressive options:

### Option 1: Challenge (Attack)
- Play **1-3 cards** from your hand
- Try to defeat the current Sentinel
- Calculate Total ATK vs Sentinel DEF
- **Important:** Cannot challenge after drawing this turn!

### Option 2: Draw Cards
- Draw **1-3 cards** from your deck
- Strengthen your hand for future turns
- **Important:** Cannot challenge after drawing!

### Option 3: Surrender
- Give up voluntarily
- Discard your hand to Jail
- You are eliminated

## Forced Elimination

If you **cannot** draw (deck empty) AND **cannot** challenge (no valid attack), you are automatically eliminated!
